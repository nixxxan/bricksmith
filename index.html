What it does

Not a chatbot that writes snippets.
A crew that ships a game.

A crew, not one prompt

A lead designer, an architect, gameplay and UI programmers, an environment artist, a 3D artist, a reviewer and a playtester. Each one gets only the context it needs.

Builds in your Studio

Bricksmith opens Roblox Studio, switches on its MCP connection and works in your place: scripts, UI, terrain, lighting and assets, with a syntax check before anything lands.

Real 3D models

For hero props it opens Blender, models the asset, and brings it into Roblox at the right size. Otherwise it uses Roblox's own AI mesh and material generation.

Ideas from the live charts

Brainstorm with an AI that reads what's trending on Roblox right now. Score your idea against the market before you build.

Playtests that actually play

The playtester walks the core loop, presses keys, clicks the UI and checks layouts for phones and gamepads, then the fixer repairs what broke.

Doesn't waste your usage

Cheaper models for simple work, the strongest model where it matters, prompt caching, small edits instead of rewrites, and filtered logs.

Stops at your limit, then carries on

When your AI plan's limit runs out, Bricksmith shows when it resets and continues by itself, even after you close the app.

Control it from anywhere

Watch the agents, approve plans, pause, or ask for changes from your phone or MacBook, on any Wi-Fi.

Launch kit

Thumbnails and icons, a shop with secure purchases, store copy and short promo clips. Then a Live tab that reads your game's numbers and tells you what to fix.

How it works

Four steps. You stay in charge.

  1. 01

    Brainstorm

    Chat about the game you want, or answer a short interview where every question has ready options or your own answer.

  2. 02

    Approve the plan

    The designer writes the game design. The architect turns it into contracts, milestones and tasks. Nothing is built until you say go.

  3. 03

    Agents build it

    They work through the tasks in Roblox Studio, playtest at every milestone and fix what fails. You see what they're thinking and writing, live.

  4. 04

    Launch and improve

    Run the launch kit, then ask the Lead for changes in plain words. Bricksmith learns from every build.

Questions

Good to know

What do I need?

A Windows 10/11 PC or a Mac, Roblox Studio, and an AI plan or API key. Blender is optional, for 3D models.

Does it use my AI subscription?

Yes. With a Claude plan it runs through Claude Code on your computer, so building uses your plan instead of paid credits. API keys stay encrypted on your computer.

Will it touch my published games?

Each new game gets its own place file. Bricksmith only works in an existing game if you import it on purpose.

What happens when I hit my usage limit?

It pauses, shows when your limit resets, and carries on from the same step automatically, even if you close and reopen the app.

Is this made by Roblox?

No. Bricksmith is an independent tool. It connects to Roblox Studio through Studio's own MCP server.
